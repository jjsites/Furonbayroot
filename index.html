Our bakery

A Beirut bakery,
made for the neighborhood.

Furon Bayroot means “Beirut ovens.” It’s the kind of corner bakery you grow up with in Lebanon: the dough is made in the morning, the oven runs hot all day, and breakfast costs a few dollars.

We bake the classics our customers ask for by name: thyme-and-oil manakish, sesame kaak stuffed with cheese, triangle spinach pies, and rolled wraps of kafta, sojok and bastirma. Stop in on your way to work, or call ahead and we’ll have it ready.
